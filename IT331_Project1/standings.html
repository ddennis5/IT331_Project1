Seahawks Fun Facts

  • Their best winning percentage is against Houston Texans at 83.3% (5-1).
  • Seattle has an all-time winning record against every NFC West team (San Francisco 49ers, Los Angeles Rams, and Arizona Cardinals).
  • The team has made four Super Bowl appearances (2005, 2014, 2015, 2025).
  • The Seahawks have won the NFC Championship four times (2005, 2013, 2014, 2025).
  • The team has made the playoffs 19 times since its inception in 1976.
  • The Seahawks' home stadium is Lumen Field, located in Seattle, Washington.
  • The Seahawks' team colors are navy blue, action green, and wolf grey.
