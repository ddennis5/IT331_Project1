About the Team

The Seattle Seahawks are a professional American football team based in Seattle, Washington, founded in 1976 and competing in the NFC West division of the National Football League (NFL).

Their home stadium is Lumen Field, located in Seattle, Washington.

The Seahawks' official team colors are navy blue, action green, and wolf grey.

Our current Head Coach is Mike Macdonald and our General Manager is John Schneider.
